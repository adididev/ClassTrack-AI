Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

ClassTrack AI uses face and voice data to mark classroom attendance. This policy explains what we store, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. We keep it short and in plain language.

In short: we store only what attendance needs, we never sell or share your data for advertising, we do not keep photos or recordings, and you can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

1. What we collect

Teachers

  • Username and name.
  • Password, stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. Nobody, including us, can read your password.
  • The subjects you create (code, name, section) and the attendance you save.

Students

  • Name.
  • Face data: a list of 128 numbers calculated from your sign-up photo (a "face embedding"). The photo itself is not stored.
  • Voice data (optional): a list of 256 numbers calculated from your voice sample (a "voiceprint"). The recording itself is not stored.
  • The subjects you enroll in and your attendance records (date, time, present or absent).

Classroom photos and recordings uploaded by teachers are processed in memory to recognize students and are then discarded. They are never saved to the database.

2. Why we use it

  • To let you log in (students by face, teachers by password).
  • To recognize enrolled students in classroom photos or recordings and mark attendance.
  • To show teachers and students their subjects and attendance history.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

3. Consent

Face and voice data are biometric data. Students are asked to agree to this policy before their profile is created, and voice enrollment is optional. You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your data (see section 7). If a student is under 18, a parent or guardian should agree on their behalf, and the school or teacher using ClassTrack AI is responsible for collecting that consent.

4. Who can see your data

  • Teachers see the names and attendance of students enrolled in their own subjects.
  • Students see their own subjects and attendance.
  • Service providers that run ClassTrack AI process data on our behalf: Supabase (database), Streamlit Community Cloud (app hosting), and Vercel (this website). This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

We share data with authorities only if the law requires it.

5. How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.
  • The database is locked down so only the app's server, using a private key, can read or write data.
  • Teachers review AI results before attendance is saved, and can correct mistakes afterwards.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account data until you ask us to delete it. When a teacher deletes a subject, its enrollments and attendance records are deleted too. Deleting a student or teacher account deletes their related records.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU GDPR, you can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us through our GitHub page. Please don't post personal details publicly; tell us how to reach you and we will follow up privately.

8. Accuracy

Face and voice recognition can make mistakes, especially in poor lighting or noisy rooms. That is why teachers confirm every result before it is saved and can correct records later.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
