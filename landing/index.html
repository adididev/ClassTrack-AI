Attendance in seconds, not minutes

ClassTrack AI marks classroom attendance automatically. Teachers snap a photo of the class or record students saying "present", and the AI recognizes each student by face or voice. The teacher reviews the result, saves it, and every session is kept in one place.

How it works

Teachers and students each have their own portal. Here is what each one does.

For teachers

  1. Create a subjectRegister, then add a subject with a code like CS101.
  2. Share the codeGive students the join link or QR code so they can enroll.
  3. Take a class photoUpload or capture photos, or record a voice clip, and run the AI.
  4. Review and saveCheck who was marked present, confirm, and see every session in Attendance Records.

For students

  1. Sign up with your faceTake one photo with your webcam. No password needed.
  2. Add your voice (optional)Record a short phrase to be recognized by voice too.
  3. Join your subjectsEnter the subject code, open the link, or scan the QR code.
  4. Track your attendanceSee every subject and how many classes you have attended.
